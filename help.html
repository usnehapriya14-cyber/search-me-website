SEARCH ME GUIDE

Let's get you job-ready.

Use this quick guide to get the most from Search Me.

01

Complete your profile

Open Resume & Skills, upload your PDF resume, and add skills such as Python, Excel, SQL, Tally, or communication.

Update resume & skills →
02

Choose your stream

Open Stream jobs and select B.Tech, B.Sc Nursing, B.Sc Agriculture, B.Com, or Any Graduate. Each stream has 50 listings.

Explore stream jobs →
03

Set your preferences

Choose government, private, remote, banking, healthcare, technology, or agriculture fields to prioritize relevant jobs.

Set preferences →
04

Apply safely

Search Me opens the official recruitment portal. Never share your OTP or password, and always review the official notification before applying.

View applications →

Check your stream, resume, and preferences first. Search Me refreshes recommendations daily, while final applications are completed on the official portal.
